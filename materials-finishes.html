Materials

Label Materials & Finishes for Food and Beverage Packaging

Material recommendations depend on package surface, moisture, oil, storage temperature, appearance and application method. Ask us to review your packaging before production.

Get a Material Recommendation
White BOPPFilm label with clean white appearance for many food and beverage packages.Often considered for moisture-resistant jar, bottle and container labels.
Clear BOPPTransparent label direction for clear bottles, jars and premium packaging.Artwork color and package surface should be reviewed before production.
PETDurable synthetic material option for selected packaging conditions.Useful when stronger film performance is preferred.
VinylFlexible synthetic option for selected durable label applications.Ask our team to confirm fit for your packaging environment.
Synthetic PaperDurable paper-like label material for packaging that needs more resistance than paper.Can be considered for refrigerated or handled packages by project.
Paper Label OptionsMatte or glossy paper labels for dry indoor packaging and natural presentation.Not recommended as a default for heavy moisture or oil exposure.
