What makes a beverage label suitable for wet handling?

Review the finished label construction on the actual bottle, cup or can through the intended process. Face stock, adhesive, printing and finish have different jobs. Record whether the surface is dry or wet when labeled, when condensation develops and whether the package flexes; then agree how the sample will be inspected. For HPP juice, decide whether the label is applied before or after processing.

Beverage Label FAQ

Questions buyers ask before ordering beverage labels

Clarify material, wet-use and roll setup before the digital proof stage.

What material is best for beverage bottle labels?

White BOPP, clear BOPP and PET film are common choices. The right option depends on bottle surface, condensation, refrigeration, artwork contrast and application method.

Can beverage labels be clear and waterproof?

Yes. Clear waterproof film can create a no-label look on transparent bottles or cups. White ink, bottle color and small text should be checked before proof approval.

What details are needed for roll beverage labels?

Send bottle or cup photos, label size, quantity, roll direction, core size if required, material preference, finish, storage condition and artwork.
