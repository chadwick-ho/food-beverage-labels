Food Packaging Labels

Tamper Seal Labels for Food Jars, Bakery Boxes and Takeaway Packaging

Seal labels help close a package, guide opening and make food packaging look finished. The key is matching shape, adhesive and surface.

Last updated: September 12, 2026 | Best for: tamper seal labels, bakery seal labels, jar lid labels, box seal stickers, food container labels and takeaway packaging labels.

Short answer

Use seal labels when a package needs a visible closing point across a lid, cap, box flap or container edge. The best design depends on the surface, opening direction, storage environment and whether the label is applied by hand or on a roll.

Practical note: a seal label can support packaging presentation, but do not rely on a generic sticker as a legal or security claim without the right packaging system and local compliance review.

Common seal label formats

FormatTypical packageDesign notes
Strip sealBakery boxes, takeaway cartons, paper bags.Needs enough length to bridge both sides of the opening.

Choose adhesive by surface

A seal label crosses edges and curves more often than a flat front label, so adhesive choice matters. Paper boxes, kraft surfaces, glossy cartons, PET cups, glass jars and chilled containers may need different adhesives. Share a package photo and storage condition before production.

Plan the shape before artwork

Seal labels should be designed around the package opening. If the sticker is too short, it will not bridge the opening. If it is too wide, it may wrinkle around a lid or curved box edge. A simple package measurement can make the first proof much closer to production-ready.

FAQ

Should seal labels be paper or film?

Paper works well for many dry boxes and bags. Film is often better for chilled, wet, oily or plastic packaging surfaces.

Can seal labels include a barcode or QR code?

They can, but small curved seals may not be the best scanning position. Use enough flat area and contrast if a code is required.

Can I order seal labels in several sizes?

Yes. Many brands use one logo style across several package sizes, but each size should be checked against the opening position.
