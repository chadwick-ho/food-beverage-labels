Custom Label MOQ: Is 100 PCS Enough for a First Packaging Run?

A 100 pcs custom label MOQ is useful when a brand needs a real packaging test before committing to bulk production.

Last updated: September 8, 2026 | Best for: food labels, beverage labels, wine labels, jar labels, bottle labels, can labels and first packaging trials.

Short answer

Yes, 100 pcs can be enough when you are testing label size, material, artwork, bottle fit, jar fit or shelf presentation. It is not always the most economical quantity per label, but it reduces risk before a brand orders 1,000 pcs, 5,000 pcs or a full production run.

Buyer decision: use 100 pcs for proofing the market and packaging fit. Move higher when the design, material and application method are confirmed.

When 100 pcs makes sense

  • You are launching a new sauce, honey, jam, drink, wine or specialty food product.
  • You need labels for samples, buyer meetings, photos, local stores or a small test sale.
  • You are still checking whether paper, white BOPP, clear BOPP or PET fits the packaging.
  • You have multiple flavors and do not want to overbuy one design too early.
  • You need a factory-made label instead of a home printer result, but the first run is still small.

Why low MOQ is useful for small food and beverage brands

Many packaging decisions become clear only after a label is applied to the real container. A flat artwork file does not show how a label handles a bottle curve, lid edge, pouch texture, chilled surface or narrow jar. A small custom label MOQ lets the buyer test the real label before scaling.

For food and beverage brands, that early test is especially important because material choice may change based on moisture, oil, refrigeration, condensation, glass curve, cup surface or freezer handling.

How to avoid wasting the first 100 pcs

Measure firstConfirm label width, height and package curve before artwork finalization.
Match materialChoose paper for dry shelf looks, film for moisture and BOPP or PET when durability matters.
Check copyReserve room for product name, flavor, net quantity, barcode and required brand information.
Proof before bulkUse the digital proof to catch size, bleed, color and layout problems.

Can several designs share one small order?

Sometimes a buyer wants 100 pcs split across flavors, scents or seasonal products. This should be quoted after the factory reviews size, material, finish and artwork differences. Designs that share the same size and material are easier to group than designs with different shapes, sizes or finishes.

What to send for a fast quote

Send packaging photos, label width and height, quantity, material preference, storage environment, application method and artwork files if available. For roll labels, also mention whether labels are hand-applied, used with a dispenser or used on a labeling machine.

FAQ

Is low MOQ lower quality?

No. MOQ is about production quantity, not automatically about quality. The more important questions are material, finish, file quality, proof approval and whether the label matches the package surface.

Should I order 100 pcs or 1,000 pcs?

Order 100 pcs if the product, artwork or material is still being tested. Order more when you already know the label fits the package and the product will be sold repeatedly.

Can I reorder after the first test?

Yes. Keep the approved size, material, finish and artwork file so reorders can be checked faster.
