Food Labels for Small Business: Packaging Checklist Before Printing

Small food brands usually do not need another vague design article. They need to know what information, label size, material and proof details must be prepared before printing custom food labels.

Last updated: September 4, 2026 | Best for: sauce jars, honey jars, bakery boxes, frozen food packs, deli containers and food pouches.

Why this topic keeps coming up

Public small-business and food founder discussions often circle around the same problems: how to make a nutrition facts panel, who is responsible for label accuracy, whether a home-printed sticker is good enough, and how to order a small batch without paying for packaging that may change after the first retail test.

For a packaging buyer, the useful answer is practical: separate the regulatory content from the physical label specification. A label factory can help with size, material, finish, roll direction, proofing and print quality. Your brand, co-packer or compliance advisor should confirm the final required wording for your market.

Fast answer: before ordering food labels, prepare your packaging photo, exact label size, product name, net quantity area, ingredient and allergen text area, nutrition panel size, barcode or QR position, storage environment and order quantity.

Compliance notes to check before final artwork

For U.S. packaged foods, FDA food labeling guidance is the correct official starting point. FDA also publishes a small business nutrition labeling exemption guide. That does not remove other required label information such as statement of identity, net quantity, ingredient statement and business name/address where applicable.

Major food allergens also need careful review. FDA guidance lists milk, egg, fish, Crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, wheat, peanuts, soybeans and sesame as major food allergens for FDA-regulated foods. Your label artwork should leave enough room for the required declaration format in your market.

FAQ

Can I print food labels at home first?

You can use a home print test to check rough size and layout, but it does not prove waterproof performance, freezer performance, barcode scan quality or production color matching. For retail use, request a material proof before scaling.

Should I choose paper or BOPP for food labels?

Choose paper for dry, premium or kraft-style packaging. Choose BOPP or PET film when the package may face moisture, refrigeration, freezing, oil or frequent handling.

Can the factory create the nutrition facts panel?

A label factory can reserve space and print the panel clearly. The actual nutrition values and required wording should be confirmed by the brand, producer or compliance professional for the destination market.
