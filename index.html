Material Guidance

Choose material by surface, storage and application

Labels for food and beverage packaging need different adhesives and films depending on moisture, oil, refrigeration, freezing and how the label will be applied. Send your packaging details and we will narrow the options before quoting.

  • Waterproof BOPP or synthetic film for bottles, cups and cold-chain packs
  • Clear BOPP or PET for transparent packaging and no-label-look designs
  • Paper labels for dry food, jars, boxes and indoor retail packaging
  • Roll or sheet format selected by hand use, dispenser or machine labeling
