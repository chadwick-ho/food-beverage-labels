Material Guidance

Not sure which material fits your packaging?

Food and beverage labels are affected by package surface, moisture, oil, condensation, refrigeration, freezing and application method. Send your packaging photo, storage environment and quantity so we can recommend a suitable direction.

  • Waterproof BOPP and synthetic options
  • Clear BOPP and PET for transparent packaging
  • Paper label options for dry indoor packaging
  • Roll or sheet format based on application method
