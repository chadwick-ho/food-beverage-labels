Waterproof, oil-resistant and cold storage options

Food labels may face moisture, oil, refrigeration, freezing, handling and curved packaging surfaces. We help recommend materials based on your packaging and storage conditions without making unsupported certification claims.

  • Moisture-resistant options for refrigerated foods
  • Cold storage material direction for frozen applications
  • Oil-resistant options for sauces and oily foods
  • Clear and white film options for different looks
