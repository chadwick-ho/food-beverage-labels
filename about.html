How to use and check our label buying guidance

Separate guidance from approval

Our buying guides help brands, packaging buyers and co-packers prepare a specification and a procurement decision. A guide does not establish that a particular adhesive, printed construction or production setting will work on your package. Confirm the selected construction, sample conditions and written approval for the actual order.

Check the source and its scope

For a technical, certification or regulatory claim, check the cited original source, its date and whether it covers the exact material, printed construction, market and use. A supplier statement for one component does not automatically cover the finished package. Ask for the applicable document when that evidence matters to your purchase.

Read examples as examples

Hypothetical scenarios and AI-generated packaging illustrations are planning aids. They do not establish a customer order, factory inspection, certification, test result or guaranteed performance. Use a physical sample of the agreed construction when appearance, fit or handling needs approval.

Keep the approval traceable

Record the container variant, artwork revision, material construction, trial conditions and remaining checks. A digital artwork proof and a physical color or performance approval answer different questions. Confirm price, order minimum, timing and documentation for the specific project in writing.
